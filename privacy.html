Privacy Policy

Social Publisher

Effective 22 September 2026 · Personal-use automation tool

Social Publisher is a personal tool built and operated by a single individual to schedule and publish their own short-form videos to their own YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram accounts. It is not offered as a service to other users, and it does not collect data from anyone other than its own operator.
01

Who this covers

This policy describes how Social Publisher handles data for its sole operator — the person who installed it and connected their own social accounts. There are no other end users; the tool runs on the operator's own computer and is not distributed publicly.

02

What it accesses on TikTok

Through TikTok's OAuth flow, the tool requests only the scopes it needs to publish video content to the operator's own account:

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which account is connected
  • video.upload and video.publish — to upload a video file and post it (or place it in the operator's TikTok inbox for manual review)

The tool never reads, follows, messages, or acts on any account other than the one the operator explicitly connects.

03

Where data lives

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in a local file on the operator's own machine. They are never transmitted to a third-party server, never sold, and never shared — the only two parties in this exchange are the operator's device and TikTok's own API.

04

What it does not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers
  • No data collected from viewers of the published videos
  • No data broker relationships of any kind
  • No account access beyond the single account the operator connects
05

Revoking access

The operator can revoke Social Publisher's access at any time from TikTok's app permissions settings, or by deleting the local token file. Either action immediately stops the tool from publishing further content.

06

Changes to this policy

If this tool's data handling changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.
