Social Publisher

Effective 22 September 2026 · Personal-use automation tool

Social Publisher is a personal automation tool, built and operated by a single individual, that schedules and publishes short-form video the operator made or holds rights to, to the operator's own YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram accounts. It is not a public service and does not accept other users.
01

What this tool does

Social Publisher reads a local queue of video files and metadata, then calls each platform's official API to publish that video to the operator's own connected account on a schedule the operator sets.

02

Single-operator scope

The tool connects to exactly one TikTok account: the operator's own. It is not distributed to, or used by, any other person. There is no multi-tenant mode, no account-switching, and no mechanism for a third party to direct what it publishes.
